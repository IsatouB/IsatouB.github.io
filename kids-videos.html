HOW THIS KEEPS HIM OFF YOUTUBE

Videos play through YouTube's no-cookie player with its own buttons switched off, and an invisible layer covers the video so taps can't reach the YouTube logo, the title, or the end-screen suggestion cards. There is no search, no comments, no sidebar, and no way to browse — only the videos you added.

Worth knowing: the video itself is still streamed by YouTube, so the picture may include anything baked into the video (Ms Rachel has ads switched off on hers). If you want fully YouTube-free playback, her show is also on Netflix.

Android: open in Chrome → menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

It then opens full screen with no address bar, so he can't type his way out. On iPhone, turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) and triple-click the side button to lock him into this one app.
